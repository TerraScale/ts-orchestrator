## Required result

[GOAL §9 D12](../../../../GOAL.md#minimum-end-to-end-acceptance-scenarios):
form the supported independent regional cells, recover from the declared
controller and quorum-loss scenarios, and verify cross-region database
behavior under the selected profile. Inter-region peer and control traffic
uses private OCI VCN routing over paired DRG remote peering with application
mTLS and signed peer identity, and fails closed. Regional NATS/JetStream
cells stay independent. Stale authority cannot mutate the deployment, and
recovery cannot silently weaken durability or residency.
