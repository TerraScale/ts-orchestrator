  and a recorded cost admission, an explicit handoff, then orchestrator-created

  five servers enrolled in NetBird and persistent across reboot; operator
  write/read through the gateway; an idempotent repeat.

  `up` and `terrabase-deploy` ran without intervention; controller A joined
  NetBird automatically. R21 recorded all five servers joining and controller A
  keeping its identity across a reboot.
