## Recommendations: fixed 10m qualification +20m aggregate forward cap

1. Enforce one **600s qualification budget** across selected suites/drivers; reject empty qualification scope. If 10m means sustained healthy observation, explicitly require that duration—smoke success and signature lifetime do not prove it. Do not restart the budget per suite/seat/retry.
2. Persist one **1200s forward deadline** for the release scope, covering upload/admission, every seat/node, retries and regions. Clamp stage waits to remaining time; publication-only CLI waits cannot enforce it. Preserve deadline across rerun/leadership change; stop advancing at expiry.
3. Keep recovery, drain safety, transport limits and credential validity distinct. Expiring forward progress must preserve safe rollback/held state, not cancel the only recovery path. Do not extend authority/signature freshness to fit observation.
4. Resolve baseline blockers first: native three-seat allowance already exceeds 20m; NetBird allows 19m **per node**; managed updates allow 15m proof **per seat**; production qualification repeats 9m boot/health per guest. Machines schemas and the ts-dashboard service cannot honestly claim this policy without their consumers/owners.

Unchecked: live configuration, external service implementations, hardware/runtime execution. Risk: reducing constants alone does not enforce a durable aggregate deadline or establish sustained qualification.
