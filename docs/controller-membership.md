# Controller membership: seed routing and break-glass refound

Every admitted production controller supervises its regional membership automatically.
The authority leader reconciles the verified slot ledger with observed NATS peers;
serving health cannot admit a slot or elect a replacement founder. Observation runs
every ten seconds, the offline grace is ten minutes, and replacement retries wait
five minutes. These policies and the retirement-evidence path are fixed in code.
Configuration files containing the obsolete `controller.membership` block are
rejected; newly rendered signed controller configs omit it.

## Seed-routed slot replacement

Only the seed controller holds the founder signer, so before this change a
lost slot was replaced only while the seed held the regional authority
lease. Any other leader held the slot as `replacer unavailable` (findings
11701 and 11995).

The seed-routed replacement path runs automatically:

- A non-seed authority leader still runs the whole decision (`Decide`).
  It then sends the action to the seed as a NATS request on
  `ts.orch.v1.<cell>.membership.replace`. The request carries:
  - the slot, its prior attempt and its prior instance;
  - the exact root-signed retirement envelope;
  - the leader's term: epoch, candidate ID and owner process ID.

  The leader renews its own lease right before it sends.
- The seed executes the action but makes no decision of its own:
  - It accepts a request only while the durable R3 lease names exactly
    that term. `authority.VerifyLeaseHolder` checks this with a read-only
    read: it never appends a lease record and never takes authority.
  - It runs the same check right before every durable or OCI mutation of
    the replacement, and polls it while the replacement runs. When the
    term ends, the replacement is cancelled.
  - It checks for itself that the prior instance is TERMINATED in OCI.
  - Its founder verifies the root signature of the retirement and the
    exact prior ledger attempt before it changes any HEAD, as before.
  - It runs one replacement at a time. Its own reconciler and routed
    requests share one serialized founder replacer.
- The reply is `accepted`, `in_progress`, `done`, `failed` (reported once,
  then the leader's retry backoff applies) or `refused`.
- On the seed, the outcome is journaled as `controller_replaced` with rule
  `membership.seed_routed`.

**Trust decision.** The seed now executes a replacement that another
controller decided. Three things authorize it:

- The NATS account: only controller users publish under the regional root.
- The R3 lease term check.
- The offline root-signed retirement for the exact instance. The delegated
  ledger signer still cannot retire.

A forged request needs a live term identity and a valid root-signed
retirement. Even then it can only trigger the same replacement the
legitimate leader would trigger, and the seed independently refuses an
instance that may still run.

**Rollout.**

- Controller credentials need the new
  `ts.orch.v1.<cell>.membership.replace` subscription grant. Material
  generated by this version includes it.
- Older material lacks the grant. The leader then gets "no responders"
  and holds the slot, as before.
- No controller enable step is required. Missing grants hold replacement safely
  and report the unavailable seed service; they never grant founder authority.

## Break-glass refound: one slot at a time

`ts-orch cluster recover-quorum refound` now refuses in three cases:

- another `refound` is running on the host (it holds the non-blocking
  `refound-pending.json.lock` flock from admission through the wait);
- another slot has an incomplete re-founded attempt in the ledger;
- the slot it re-founded before has not reported current.

It records the slot it relaunches in
`/var/lib/terrascale-orchestrator-controller/membership/refound-pending.json`.
The record must contain the exact region, slot, matching server name, successor
attempt and launch time. Malformed, writable, linked or foreign-region records
block recovery, including after restart. After the launch, it waits (up to 30 minutes) until the running survivor's
cluster view shows that slot online and caught up. "Caught up" needs
positive evidence: the metadata leader reports the peer current with zero
lag (or, from a follower's view, the peer is the leader it heard from
recently), in a view observed after the relaunch began, because the
successor reuses the lost instance's server name. Merely not being listed
as lagging is not enough. If the survivor is a follower and the re-founded
slot is another follower, the survivor cannot vouch for it and the wait
times out. If the wait times out, the record stays, so rerun `refound` for
the same slot to keep waiting.

A production controller writes its cluster view every health tick to
`/var/lib/terrascale-orchestrator-controller/membership/cluster-health.json`
(owner-only). The file is informational: the controller never reads it
back.

## Remaining work

- A long-lived, root-signed, slot-scoped replacement authorization that does
  not depend on the founding plan's `LaunchDeadline` (finding 11704), and a
  seed-handoff path when the seed slot itself is lost.
- A production `placement.LostMemberEvidence` source from `oci.Fleet`
  writer-stop and detach proof.
